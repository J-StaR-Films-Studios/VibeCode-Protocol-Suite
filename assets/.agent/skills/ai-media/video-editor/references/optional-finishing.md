# Optional finishing

Use only for finishing work included in the current brief. Visual editing does not automatically authorize new music, SFX, voice treatment or stylistic grading. Preserve existing user processing.

## Speech and audio

Use the linked source speech for transcription, preferably before added music. Preserve the master. Check source selection, sync and natural joins after cuts even when sound design is not requested.

When audio finishing is authorized, listen before adjusting noise reduction, EQ, dynamics or level. Avoid thin, metallic speech. Do not reuse numeric settings from a previous project or infer a plugin from an ambiguous sound description. Compare quiet and emphatic passages and check pumping, distortion and abrupt joins. Meters support listening; they do not replace it.

Inspect available music before sourcing another track. When the user requests music and authorizes public-source acquisition, research and audition accessible candidates, check the exact track's licence, download permitted material and build the music bed. Do not deliver only track names or search phrases when the assignment is to finish the edit. If audition or acquisition is unavailable, state the specific limit rather than claiming a track was heard or approved.

Select music for the commercial mood, speech rhythm and arrangement, not merely a tempo tag. Keep the original dialogue intelligible and use level automation where needed. Preserve the original file, title, artist, source URL, licence evidence and required attribution. A library being described as free does not establish rights for every track. Distinguish the original from excerpts and edited beds. Arrange repeats around musical phrases and avoid audible seams or unintended tails. Music authorization does not automatically authorize SFX, speech processing, paid subscriptions or private-media uploads.

Add SFX only within the brief. Do not create a sound stem merely because a graphic moves. When external graphics should be silent, verify that their exports contain no audio streams. A speech preview must not double the original dialogue.

Fairlight plugin settings and automation may not be available through the exposed tools. An unsupported property returning false does not establish a zero level. Say what can be read back and what needs listening. Do not claim an effect adjustment or mix review succeeded without supporting evidence.

## Colour

Inspect actual frames and existing grading before making changes. Correct exposure, white balance and distracting casts when authorized. Protect skin tones, match shots and avoid clipped highlights or crushed faces. Show a representative before/after before broad stylistic grading. A crop of a title cannot establish the quality of the whole image.

## Final review

Review the rendered result, not merely source assets or timeline settings. Check continuity, sync, speech, requested music/SFX, graphic timing and any captions across the finished piece. Verify dimensions, frame rate, duration and expected audio/video streams. Check clipping when audio finishing is in scope.

State plainly if full playback, listening or editor-import testing could not be performed. Preserve editable work and stable media paths. If the user will export, leave the timeline ready and identify unverified render settings. Do not claim a master was delivered without an actual file.
