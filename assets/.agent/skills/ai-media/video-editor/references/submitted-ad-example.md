# Worked example: a short creative-services ad

## Evidence

This example comes from the user's submitted Black Friday and Cyber Monday edit, reviewed during development of this skill. The export was approximately 27 seconds at 1080 by 1920 and 30 fps. The review used timestamped frames, fine temporal samples and read-only Resolve timeline data, including clip-enabled states. It did not establish a full real-time viewing or listening review.

The summary below is self-contained. No private footage, credentials or absolute client paths are bundled. Times are approximate positions in this one export, not timing defaults. This example does not prove campaign performance.

## Choices visible in the submitted cut

| Passage and approximate time | What the user chose | What to carry into another edit |
|---|---|---|
| Opening, 0 to 3.6 seconds | MORE CONTENT graphics above a populated scrolling social grid, followed by the speaker again | Graphics can state the idea while footage demonstrates it in the same composition |
| Time cue, 4.80 to 5.13 seconds | About ten frames of a macro wristwatch with visibly moving hands | A brief associative action can be enough; a full countdown is not automatically required |
| Creative-partner introduction | A-roll with a small label instead of the large partner/stopwatch module | The speaker and modest text can carry an explanation without another board |
| Services, 8.8 to 12.9 seconds | Storyboard, product shoot, editing workstation and handshake, with SCRIPT / SHOOT / EDIT labels | Show a process through footage; the handshake represents collaboration, not the act of a creator recording |
| Static designs, roughly 13 to 14.5 seconds | A finished PRODUCT SPOTLIGHT image ad entering over the speaker near a presenting hand | Demonstrate the deliverable; animate its presentation rather than replace it with an image icon |
| Reassurance, roughly 14.5 to 18.4 seconds | Speaker-led passage followed by selective KEEP YOUR TEAM graphics and reframing | Use expression and supporting graphics together for reassurance |
| Amplify and scale, roughly 18.4 to 23 seconds | The approved creative-card staircase with the speaker underneath | A diagram earns its place when it explains a progression |
| CTA, roughly 23 to 27 seconds | Speaker-led setup, then WATCH THE VIDEO and BOOK A CALL in sequence | Clear one action before presenting the next |

## Earlier proposals that missed the brief

The initial graphics pack extended one approved card style across almost every passage. It used a stationary stopwatch and illustrative placeholders for services. The user liked the scale animation but expected more editorial selection, actual examples and sourcing help.

The submitted timeline retained useful graphics and disabled the service board at clip level. It replaced that board with footage. This is why inspecting only track state or filenames would have produced the wrong conclusion.

## Limits on generalization

Do not copy the whole split-screen layout, warm background, yellow accents, product category, handshake or rapid cuts into the next project. Do not assume visible feed counters substantiate results or that generated ads are verified client work. Approval of the edit does not verify those implications.

The transferable lesson is the selection process: decide the visual job, choose a suitable medium or combination, make any intended action visible, and return to the speaker when delivery matters. Keep sound design out of the default scope, as the user requested.
