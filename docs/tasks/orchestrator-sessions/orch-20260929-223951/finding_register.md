# Findings from the Takomi and Codex source audits

## Disposition rules
This register captures the parent audit and all three specialist reports from the preceding conversation. Entries are leads until verified against the isolated baseline. Initial dispositions describe what to do next, not accepted implementation decisions. Use fix now, investigate first, correct docs, or defer with a reason. Keep unsupported claims and recommendations separate from confirmed defects. The final status must include evidence, task or owner, verification, and remaining limitations.
